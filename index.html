Privacy Policy for PixelPinch Last updated: 3 September 2026 Overview PixelPinch is developed by Jiten Labs. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does not. Your photos stay on your device All image processing — resizing, cropping, compression, rotation, and metadata removal — happens entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to any server, never sent to us, and never shared with any third party. PixelPinch has no cloud storage and no backend. No account required PixelPinch does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. We do not collect, store, or have access to any personal data about you. Advertising PixelPinch displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and process: - Your device's advertising ID - Device information such as model, operating system version, and language - General location inferred from IP address - Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks This data is collected by Google, not by us. We do not receive your personal information from Google. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads. Consent for users in the EU, UK, and certain regions If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region with applicable privacy laws, PixelPinch will ask for your consent before serving personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You may change your choice at any time through the app. Permissions PixelPinch requests the following: - Photos and media — to let you select images to resize and to save processed images back to your device. Used only when you choose a photo or save a result. - Camera — only if you choose to take a new photo within the app. - Internet — used solely to load advertisements. Your images are never transmitted. Children PixelPinch is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
